My Favorite Books

  • To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee - A classic novel that explores themes of racial injustice and moral growth in the American South.
  • 1984 by George Orwell - A dystopian novel that delves into the dangers of totalitarianism and government surveillance.
  • Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen - A romantic novel that critiques the social norms and expectations of 19th-century England.
  • The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald - A story about the American Dream, wealth, and social change during the Roaring Twenties.
  • The Catcher in the Rye by J.D. Salinger - A coming-of-age novel that explores themes of identity, alienation, and adolescence.
